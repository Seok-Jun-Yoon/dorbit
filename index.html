Biography

About Greyfield

Greyfield is an indie-pop duo known for atmospheric production, sharp songwriting, and a live show built for rooms of every size. Since forming, the band has released two EPs and toured markets across the country, sharing stages with a rotating cast of like-minded acts.

Rooted in melody but unafraid of space, Greyfield's sound sits at the intersection of bedroom pop intimacy and festival-stage scale — quiet enough for headphones, big enough for a room to sing along.
